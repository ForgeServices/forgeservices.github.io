    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    How do I place an order?

    Choose a service and tap Contact to Order, or build a cart. Telegram opens with a draft message. You review it and press Send yourself.

    Why aren’t all prices listed?

    Website packages show starting prices. Other services vary by project, so FORGE sends you a quote on Telegram.

    Can I order something that isn’t listed?

    Yes. Use Request a Custom Quote and describe your project.

    How long does delivery take?

    Each service shows an estimated delivery time. The exact timeline is agreed with you on Telegram.

    Does this site take payments?

    No. The cart is a summary to review. Nothing is paid or confirmed here.

    Is a Telegram message an order?

    It becomes an order request only after you send it and FORGE acknowledges it.

    How much does a website cost?

    Packages start at $100 (Starter, 1–3 pages) and go to $400+ for custom projects. The final price is confirmed in your written proposal.

    Are domain and hosting included?

    No. Domain registration, hosting, paid themes or plugins and third-party subscriptions are paid separately.

    What does the Payment Guide & Calculator do?

    It produces an indicative price in USD from your requirements. It is an estimate only and does not process payments.

    What is the Project Contract tool?

    It generates a printable website agreement draft from your project details. It does not provide verified e-signatures, secure storage or legal certification.

    How many revisions are included?

    Starter 1 round, Business 3, Premium 5, and Custom as agreed in writing. Revisions are changes within the agreed scope, not new pages or features.

    Do you guarantee sales or search rankings?

    No. We deliver the agreed work. Business results, traffic and rankings depend on factors outside our control.

    Trust & Policies

    Terms, privacy and project policies

    These are plain-language summaries of how FORGE works with clients. They are drafts that have not been reviewed by a lawyer. Have them reviewed for the jurisdictions you serve before relying on them as binding documents. FORGE does not claim a registered company, office address or certification on this site.

    Terms of Service

    FORGE provides digital services (websites, design, video, AI images, social media services and digital tasks) as described in the quote or agreement for each project. A quote is not binding until scope, price, timeline and payment terms are confirmed in writing. This website does not take orders or payments by itself. Clients must have the rights to content they supply and may not request unlawful work. Social media services depend on third-party platforms whose rules FORGE does not control, so results and retention are not guaranteed.

    Privacy Policy

    This site has no accounts. Your cart and any inquiry draft are kept only in your browser session so you do not lose them, and the inquiry draft is cleared after a successful submission. Details you submit reach FORGE through the channel you use: a Telegram draft that you send yourself, or a form service if one is enabled on this site. We ask only for what we need to reply. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. Never send passwords, login codes or card details. To ask FORGE to delete details you have sent, message us on Telegram.

    Refund & Cancellation

    Either side may request cancellation in writing. The client pays for work completed and non-cancellable costs incurred up to that point. Any unused prepaid amount is reconciled fairly against work and costs, subject to applicable law and mandatory consumer rights. Exact terms for a project are set in its agreement.

    Project Scope & Revision Policy

    Each project has an agreed scope of pages, features and deliverables. Revisions are adjustments within that scope: Starter includes 1 round, Business 3, Premium 5, and Custom as agreed in writing. New pages, new features or a full redesign are extra work, quoted and approved in writing before they begin. Report issues against the agreed scope within 7 days of receiving the preview.

    Payment Terms

    Payment uses a method agreed with you; PayPal and USDT are the options currently listed on this site. A deposit is due before work begins and the balance after preview approval and before final transfer or launch, unless agreed otherwise in writing. Nothing is paid through this website, and the Payment Guide & Calculator and Project Contract are resources, not payment or signing systems. Confirm payment details directly with FORGE before paying. FORGE never asks for your passwords.

    Ownership & Final Deliverables

    After full payment, the client receives rights to the custom final deliverables created for the project, subject to third-party software, fonts, stock assets and open-source licenses. FORGE keeps its pre-existing tools, reusable code and know-how, and may show completed work in its portfolio unless agreed otherwise in writing.
